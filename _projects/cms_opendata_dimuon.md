These datasets represent a processed subset of the CMS Open Data corresponding to 2012 data-taking conditions ($4.429~\text{fb}^{-1}$ of $pp$ collisions at $\sqrt{s}=8$ TeV), specifically optimized for education and outreach dimuon physics exercises.

### Features

- **Formats**: Available in both **CSV** and **Pickle (PKL)** formats.
- **Data subset**: `DoubleMuParked` primary dataset from CMS Run 2012B (`/DoubleMuParked/Run2012B-22Jan2013-v1/AOD`).
- **Simulation subset**: `DYJetsToLL` reduced NanoAOD Monte Carlo sample ($30,458,871$ generated events).
- **Processing**: Produced with a modified version of the [AOD2NanoAODOutreachTool](https://github.com/cms-opendata-analyses/AOD2NanoAODOutreachTool).
